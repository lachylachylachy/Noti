# Boards Workspace

## Purpose

Boards provide a structured visual interpretation of project threads.

They should be useful when work needs status, grouping, or flow.

## Core Direction

Boards should be one possible render mode, not the main identity of Noti.

A board may show threads grouped by:

- status
- project
- priority
- stage
- workspace

## UI Direction

Borrow clarity from Trello/Linear, but avoid dense operational clutter.

Boards should feel calm, spacious, and optional.

## What to Avoid

Avoid making everything a card.

Avoid forcing board workflows onto simple notes or personal thoughts.
